02 How we work

Each layer answers a question
the next one cannot start without.

Layer1

Discovery

What is actually true here?

We audit the market and your competitors, interview your customers, and pressure-test your offer against what we find — naming the problem before anyone proposes a solution.

You get
  • Where the market actually places you
  • What your customers say, in their own words
  • Where your offer and your pitch disagree
  • The number everything afterwards is measured against

If it turns out you don’t have a brand problem, that is what the document will say.

You sign off on the diagnosis
Layer2

Brand strategy

So what do we decide?

We turn the diagnosis into decisions — who this is for, what it is worth choosing over — then set three or four pillars: the points your brand makes everywhere, each tied to something Layer 1 found.

You get
  • Who this is for
  • What you are choosing against
  • The three or four points you make everywhere
  • The language to make them in

From here on, every piece of work can be judged against something instead of argued about.

You lock the strategy
Layer3

Marketing & tech execution

Now, what do we build?

We turn each pillar into briefs, then run them — campaigns, website, systems — checking every piece against the strategy before it reaches you.

You get
  • Campaigns, websites and systems
  • Each one traceable to a decision above it
